About

The person behind the wave

I'm Mitchell, a designer who codes, based near Amsterdam. I started out in Technical Computer Science, moved over to Communication & Multimedia Design, and never really picked a side. Interfaces, front-end and the photography that fills them all end up on the same desk.

That mix is the whole point. Because I build what I design, the concept and the thing that actually ships never drift apart. Alongside the studio I'm a frontend developer intern at Build in Amsterdam (BiA), where I get to sharpen my craft on real work.
