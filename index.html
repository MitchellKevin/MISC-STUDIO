How it works

How does a project start?

With a free, no-obligation intro call. We talk about what you need, who it is for, and your budget and timeline. After that I write down the scope and send a fixed quote.

Who will I be working with?

With me, Mitchell. I design and build every project myself, so the person you brief is the person who makes it.

How much say do I have in the design?

A lot. Flows and wireframes come first, then the full design in Figma. You get two rounds of feedback before anything is built.

Pricing

What does a website cost?

A small project starts at €1,500 and a larger one at €5,000. If it doesn’t fit either, we work out the scope together and you get a price on request.

Is the price fixed?

Yes. I write down what gets built, when, and for how much. You get a fixed quote before any work starts.

Launch & after

Can I edit the site myself?

Larger projects can come with a CMS (Storyblok), so you can change text and images yourself. At handover I include CMS training if you need it.

What happens after launch?

The site goes live and you get the keys. I stay available for one week after launch to fix anything that comes up.

Will it work on phones and be secure?

Every build is tested on phone, tablet and desktop, with accessibility and security checked along the way. Inputs get validated and dependencies stay up to date.
