Overview

Shot for the layout, not for the archive.

Because I build the pages the photos land on, the shot list starts from the layout: what has to be a wide hero, what needs room for text over it, what has to survive a square crop on a phone.

You get edited, colour-graded files in the sizes and formats the site actually uses, plus the raw selects, with the licensing written down.

What's included

Everything the engagement covers

Product Photography

Clean, consistent shots of the thing you sell, on a set built for repeatability.

Brand & Editorial

Atmosphere and context. The images that set the tone for everything else.

Events & On-location

Coverage on the day, wherever it happens, without staging the room to death.

Portraits

Team and founder portraits that look like the people, not like stock.

Editing & Grading

A consistent look across the set, so the site never reads as a collage.

Delivery & Licensing

Web-ready exports at the right sizes, with usage rights agreed up front.

Process

How I work

01 Brief I go through where the images will live and what each one has to do.
02 Shot list A concrete list per page slot, so nothing important is discovered on set.
03 Shoot Half a day or a full day, on location or on a simple built set.
04 Select & edit You pick from a proof gallery; the selects get the full grade and retouch.
05 Deliver Exports in every size the site needs, plus originals and the licence.
